The short version

We only use what you send us to answer your property enquiry and connect you with an agent. We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Groot Ops AI ("Groot Ops", "we", "us") builds and operates AI assistants for real estate agencies. Our assistants answer property enquiries on channels such as WhatsApp, qualify leads, offer viewing times and help agents with follow-ups and paperwork.

When an assistant works on behalf of an agency, that agency decides why your information is used and Groot Ops processes it for them. When you use this website or contact Groot Ops directly, Groot Ops is responsible for your information.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name shown on WhatsApp, and any name or email you give us.
  • Your messages: the text you send to an assistant, including what you are looking for, such as area, property type, budget, timeline and preferred viewing times.
  • Lead records: a short summary of your enquiry, the property it matches and the agent assigned to you.
  • Technical data: message identifiers, timestamps and delivery status from WhatsApp, and basic server logs.
  • Account data: for agency staff using our dashboard, your name, email and a securely hashed password.

Please don't send payment details, bank account numbers or identity documents through our assistants. They are designed not to ask for them.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and keep the conversation going.
  • To match you with suitable listings and arrange viewings with an agent.
  • To keep the agency's lead records, follow-ups and reports up to date.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

4. Who processes it

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the service:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between you and the assistant.
  • AI model providers (for example Anthropic): generate the assistant's replies from your messages.
  • Google Workspace: stores lead records, viewing briefs and draft emails for the agency.
  • Hosting providers: our servers and website run on cloud infrastructure, including Vercel.
  • The real estate agency you contacted, whose agents follow up with you.

Each provider only receives what it needs to do its part. Some providers are located outside your country; where that happens we rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect your information.

5. AI assistants

The assistant you chat with on WhatsApp is automated. It can make mistakes, so it never confirms prices, legal or title details, or bookings by itself. An agent always confirms viewings and handles anything involving money or documents.

6. How long we keep it

We keep conversations and lead records for as long as the agency needs them to handle your enquiry, and for no more than 24 months after your last message, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also stop messaging the assistant at any time.

How to delete your data

Send a WhatsApp message saying "delete my data", or email us at {{ contact_email }} from your phone number or with it included. We will delete your conversation and lead records within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

If you are in Nigeria, you also have rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We encrypt data in transit with HTTPS, restrict access to the people and systems that need it, keep secret keys out of our code, and check that messages really come from WhatsApp before acting on them.

9. Children

Our services are for adults looking to rent, buy or sell property. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. We will change the date at the top of this page whenever we do.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email {{ contact_email }}.
